Our Story

A Neighbourhood
Institution

Since 2009, Four Ate Five has been Surry Hills' favourite morning ritual. Born from a love of Jewish grandmother cooking, our Israeli-inspired menu celebrates bold flavours, honest ingredients, and the joy of a great brunch shared with good people.

  • Handmade pita, falafel & homemade preserves daily
  • Single O coffee — Sydney's finest specialty roaster
  • Renovated 2015 with open-plan Israeli heritage design
  • Local Surry Hills artworks displayed throughout
  • Dine-in, takeaway & catering for all occasions
